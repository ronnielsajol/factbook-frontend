School Safety & Student Support — prepared public data
Prepared 11 September 2026.

CSV percentages use 0–100; monetary amounts are PHP. Budget quarters are quarter-only, snapshots are year-to-date. Use one report vintage per fiscal year. 2025 full-year and 2026 half-year rates are not equivalent-period comparisons. Counselor data is a dated two-role conversion-pool baseline subject to HR validation, not current support coverage.

Sources.csv provides direct official URLs and hashes. Original PDFs are not included in this web download.

The JSON includes survey design, source provenance, financial revisions and scope limitations. Never add overlapping survey summaries or combine survey percentages with infrastructure enrollment.
