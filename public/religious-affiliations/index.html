Religious Affiliation in the Philippines

Release Date: Wed, 02/22/2023

Source: Philippine Statistics Authority

Definition of Terms

Religious affiliation refers to how members of the household population identified their religion in the 2020 Census.

The PSA conducts data collection by household, meaning religious affiliation is reported using household population as the base.

This covers individuals living in regular households and excludes those in institutional or collective living arrangements.
